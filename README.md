## 📖 Quick Overview
Written by **Ken Wakui**, *Tokyo Revengers* follows **Takemichi Hanagaki**, a ~~powerless~~ determined 26-year-old who time-leaps **12 years into the past** to infiltrate the **Tokyo Manji Gang (Toman)** and save his ex-girlfriend, **Hinata Tachibana**, from a tragic future.

## 🎭 Core Characters
| Character | Role | Known For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Takemichi Hanagaki** | 1st Div. Captain | Time-leaper (*"Crybaby Hero"*) |
| **Manjiro Sano (Mikey)** | Toman President | Undefeated fighter (*"Invincible Mikey"*) |
| **Ken Ryuguji (Draken)** | Toman Vice-President | Mikey's right-hand man |
